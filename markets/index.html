The price of one troy ounce of gold in US dollars. Sensitive to real yields, the dollar, risk sentiment and central-bank demand.

EURUSD

Euro / US Dollar

The most traded currency pair. Driven by the policy gap between the ECB and the Federal Reserve and by relative growth.

GBPUSD

British Pound / US Dollar

Cable. Reacts to Bank of England decisions, UK inflation and labour data, and broad US dollar moves.

USDJPY

US Dollar / Japanese Yen

Closely tied to the yield gap between US Treasuries and Japanese government bonds, and to Bank of Japan policy.

BTCUSD

Bitcoin / US Dollar

Trades around the clock and is highly volatile. Influenced by liquidity conditions, flows and regulation.
