Gold and FX: read the policy message before choosing a direction

A practical framework for interpreting the coming policy and data releases, without invented price targets.

Published 25 October 2026 at 23:41 Istanbul1 min readUpdated 26 October 2026 at 00:06 Istanbul
XAUUSDNeutral

Information as of 25 October 2026 at 23:41 Istanbul

This analysis may no longer reflect current conditions. Check the publication date.

Start with what is verified

This week combines a Fed decision, an ECB decision, and US growth, spending and employment-cost releases. The order matters: Thursday’s data arrives after Wednesday’s Fed decision and can change how that message is interpreted.

Gold: follow the transmission channel

The Chicago Fed identifies real interest rates, inflation expectations and concern about future economic conditions as important gold-price drivers. A policy surprise matters through these channels, not just through whether the announced rate rises or stays unchanged.

FX: compare two policy paths

Our analytical framework for EUR/USD is relative: a change in the US outlook matters alongside the euro-area outlook. For the other major dollar pairs, distinguish a broad dollar reaction from news specific to the other currency. Do not turn an initial spike into proof of a lasting trend.

What would change the reading?

If the first market reaction reverses as the press conference continues, reassess the initial interpretation. No technical support, resistance or entry levels are published here because a current price series has not been verified.
