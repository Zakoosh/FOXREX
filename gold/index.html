What moves gold

Real yields

Gold pays no interest, so higher inflation-adjusted yields raise the cost of holding it.

The US dollar

Gold is priced in dollars; a stronger dollar often weighs on it.

Risk sentiment

Stress and uncertainty can lift demand for gold as a store of value.

Central banks
