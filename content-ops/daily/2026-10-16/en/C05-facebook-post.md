**Why does gold care about inflation reports?**

This week's US CPI (3.7% y/y vs 3.6% expected, core 2.6% vs 2.5%) lifted talk of tighter Fed policy and pushed the dollar to its highest since April 2025 before it pared gains.

Gold pays no interest, so when rate expectations rise, holding it gets relatively costlier. Yet gold has stayed above $4,100 — a reminder that one data point rarely decides the story.
