Four central banks, one week — and the order of events matters.

Before you look at any chart this week, put the calendar in order:
1️⃣ Wed Oct 28: Fed decision and press conference
2️⃣ Thu Oct 29: ECB decision, plus US GDP and PCE (reported)
3️⃣ Fri Oct 30: Bank of Japan (reported)

Why it matters: a headline can move prices in seconds, but the *message* — the tone, the guidance — often takes the whole press conference to read. A first candle is not a verdict.

Our habit: write down what would change your view BEFORE the event, not after.
