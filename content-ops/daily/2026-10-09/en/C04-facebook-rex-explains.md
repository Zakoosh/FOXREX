**REX explains: why does gold dislike high yields?**

Gold pays no interest. When safe government bonds pay more (the 10-year hovered near 5.2–5.3% this week, per secondary sources), holding gold costs you the interest you could have earned. A stronger dollar also makes gold pricier for non-US buyers.

That is a general mechanism, not a prediction. Many other factors move gold too.
