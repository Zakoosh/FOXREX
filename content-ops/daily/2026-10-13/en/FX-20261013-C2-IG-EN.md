Strong dollar = weak gold? 🦊

REX explains in 30 seconds:
1) Gold is priced in dollars, so a stronger dollar makes it pricier for holders of other currencies.
2) Gold pays no interest, so higher yields raise the cost of holding it.

The dollar hit an 18-month high on 12 Oct and gold has been under pressure. It's a tendency, not a rule — other forces can outweigh it.
