Why does a stronger dollar often weigh on gold?

A common question this week, with the dollar at an 18-month high (12 Oct) and gold trading under pressure.

Two mechanisms traders point to:
1. Currency effect — gold is quoted in US dollars, so when the dollar rises, gold costs more in euros, yen or lira, which can dampen demand.
2. Opportunity cost — gold pays no yield, so rising yields make holding it less attractive relative to bonds.

Caveats: this is a tendency, not a law. Safe-haven demand, central-bank buying or geopolitics can push the other way. CPI on Wednesday at 15:30 Istanbul is the next catalyst to watch.
