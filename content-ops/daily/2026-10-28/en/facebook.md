Why does the same Fed decision move gold one way for some traders and the other way for others?
Because markets trade the *difference between what's expected and what's said*. Today, reports say a hold is widely expected, so attention turns to guidance about December and to the press conference that follows the statement.
Gold has been reported around the $4,050 area, with analysts watching $4,000–$3,950 as support — but these are snapshots from secondary reports, not live quotes, and the outcome is unknown.
