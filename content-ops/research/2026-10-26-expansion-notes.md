# FOXREX content expansion · 26 October 2026

Owner mandate: 20 additional news originals, 20 technical analyses and a more interactive REX classroom. Each original has Arabic, English and Turkish editions. News are contextual reviews of verified prior official releases; dates are explicit in article bodies, source labels and category cards. They are not labelled breaking news. Topic tags drive populated filters, with full-text title/summary search and progressive display of nine articles at a time. The complete static links remain available without JavaScript.

## Data and visuals

`2026-10-26-news-evidence.json` records original release dates, links, facts and original conditional commentary. The 20 covers are distinct editorial illustrations, not evidence photos or charts. BLS rolling release URLs must be checked against the dated evidence when updated later.

`data/market/2026-10-26-expansion.json` contains the actual chart inputs, source URLs, retrieval times, omitted inconsistent observations and reproducible metrics. Gold and silver W1 aggregate four complete Monday–Friday weeks from the prior checked daily observations. They have no RSI or moving averages because the sample is too short. The 18 D1 series use 70 valid observations, EMA20 seeded at the first close and Wilder RSI14. Invalid bars are omitted rather than repaired. FX session labels follow Yahoo's Europe/London timezone; the latest FX session is 23 October. Crypto uses completed UTC days through 24 October. Charts show candles and calculated classic pivot levels, never forecast bars. Indicators and pivots are historical reference calculations, not execution prices or guarantees.

Each original has a distinct cover; language siblings intentionally share that original’s visual. New REX teacher and celebrating poses preserve the navy/emerald identity and transparency. Alongside the existing portrait, they form the three selectable character modes. The pose may react to quiz feedback but is not a live chat or personalised advice system.

## Classroom behaviour

Five two-option questions provide immediate explanations. Explicit lesson-completion buttons and quiz answers are stored only on the visitor's device under `foxrex-rex-progress-v3`, shared across languages, with a reset control. The hypothetical risk calculator validates positive inputs and opposing stop/target sides. It assumes one currency unit per point per unit and excludes fees, gaps, slippage and instrument-specific point values; it does not calculate broker lots.
