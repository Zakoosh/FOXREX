Weekend brief: the verified week-ahead agenda for gold and FX

Sunday 25 October: prepare for central-bank decisions and US inflation and growth data. No live quotes are used.

Published 25 Oct 2026, 23:41 IST · By FOXREX Desk · AI-assisted editorial

Information as of 25 Oct 2026, 23:41 IST

This Morning Brief is from 25 Oct 2026, 23:41 IST. Its levels and conditions may no longer apply.

Confirmed schedule — Istanbul time
The Federal Reserve decision is scheduled for Wednesday 28 October at 21:00, followed by its press conference at 21:30. Thursday 29 October brings the US advance GDP estimate and September Personal Income and Outlays at 15:30. The ECB decision follows at 16:15. US employment costs are scheduled for Friday at 15:30.

Desk interpretation
For gold, distinguish the policy decision from the subsequent message. For EUR/USD, compare Fed and ECB guidance rather than treating either announcement in isolation. GBP/USD and USD/JPY can also react to changes in the dollar and US yields; other domestic releases are outside this selected calendar.

Conditional scenarios
A message that reduces expected policy restraint could help gold if real yields ease. A more restrictive message could work in the opposite direction. These are analytical scenarios, not a statement of current market pricing.

Data note
This is a weekend planning brief. Live gold and FX prices and consensus estimates have not been verified and are omitted. Consult the calendar and official sources for changes.
